# One employee remembers: the person's checklist for notes, the handbook, the cost and the Keychain

> Snapshot from 2026-10-20: the steps a person at the screen runs to prove an employee remembers, follows the handbook, costs what Usage says, pauses on a budget and keeps its keys in the Keychain, in the real window. Every result below is awaiting the person; nothing here was run with a signed-in service. Not maintained.

## 1. What is wired

One `Session` per helper generation builds the inbox, usage and models stores once and hands them to every screen that needs one: the Inbox screen and its budget card's Resume use the session's usage store, the Settings window's Models and Usage panes read `session.models` and `session.usage`, and each bot's inspector reads its notes and handbook stores, kept per bot on the session. The Access sheet takes its Advanced model choice from `session.models`. All of it is dropped with the session when the helper goes. The key store (`CredentialSync`) runs on each helper generation and answers the helper's credential events.

## 2. The person's checklist

Run from a bundle built with `make app-build`, on a throwaway data folder for steps 2 to 10 (`open --env HEARTH_DATA_DIR=<folder>`), signed in to a service, with a bot named Dana hired. Step 1 is the exception: it needs the data folder of an install from before the update, so it is run on a copy of one. Record each result in a new dated note, not by editing this one. Time each step from the action to the thing expected; the timing field is for that number.

1. **The Keychain after the update.** Launch the new build over a data folder that still holds `keys/` and `chatgpt/`. Expect: the first launch moves the keys into the Keychain (the login keychain for an ad-hoc build); `keys/` and `chatgpt/` are deleted from the data folder only after the second sync, so quit, launch again and then look. Open Keychain Access first and confirm the records are there before judging the files. Expect: records present, then both folders gone, and every service still signed in. Timing: awaiting the person. Result: awaiting the person.
2. **A handbook page.** Open Dana's inspector, write a handbook page ("always sign emails with the team name") and save. Ask Dana to draft an email. Expect: the page is used without being asked for. Timing: awaiting the person. Result: awaiting the person.
3. **A preference becomes a note.** Tell Dana a preference ("I like meetings before noon"). Expect: a note appears in her inspector within the reply, and it survives selecting someone else and coming back. Timing: awaiting the person. Result: awaiting the person.
4. **A $1 budget, a pause, Resume.** Set Dana's budget to $1, keep asking until the run pauses. Expect: a pause card in the thread and a budget item in the Inbox; the sidebar marks her paused. Choose Resume on the Inbox card. Expect: the item closes, the pause mark clears and the usage figures match the Usage pane. Timing: awaiting the person. Result: awaiting the person.
5. **Earlier in this conversation.** Hold a long thread until the history no longer fits, then ask about its first topic. Expect: the transcript shows "Earlier in this conversation" and Dana still answers from the summary. Timing: awaiting the person. Result: awaiting the person.
6. **A dropped text file.** Drag a text file onto the composer and ask about it. Expect: the file shows as a chip, drop is accepted (the composer highlights while the file is over it), and Dana answers from its contents. Timing: awaiting the person. Result: awaiting the person.
7. **Space on a chip.** Select a file chip in the composer or the thread and press Space. Expect: a Quick Look preview of the file opens, and Space again closes it. Timing: awaiting the person. Result: awaiting the person.
8. **Best to Ollama.** With Ollama running, open Settings > Models and set Best to an Ollama model, then ask Dana something that needs Best. Expect: the pane shows the choice and the reply comes from that model. Where the service card has no model list, type the model id. Timing: awaiting the person. Result: awaiting the person.
9. **The Access sheet's Advanced pin.** Open Access (Option-Command-A), open Advanced and pin Dana to one model. Expect: the pin is saved, Dana's replies use it, and clearing the pin returns her to her tier. Timing: awaiting the person. Result: awaiting the person.
10. **The cost against Usage.** After the steps above, compare the cost in Dana's inspector, the Usage pane and the Inbox figures. Expect: the same number everywhere. Timing: awaiting the person. Result: awaiting the person.
